Copy on Select – Auto Copy · Last updated 2026-10-20

Copy on Select is a Safari extension that copies text you select with the mouse to your Mac's clipboard.

What Copy on Select collects

Nothing. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

What it handles on your Mac

  • The text you select. Copied to your Mac's clipboard and nowhere else. Copy on Select keeps no copy and no history.
  • Your settings. Whether it's on, the websites you've turned it off for, and your two options are saved in Safari's extension storage on your Mac. They are not sent anywhere.

Website access

Safari asks you to allow Copy on Select on websites. It needs this to see the text you select. It only reads your selection, when you finish selecting with the mouse. It does not read or send the rest of the page.
