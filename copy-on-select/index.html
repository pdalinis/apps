Copy on Select

Auto Copy · Safari extension for Mac

Select text in Safari and it's already on your clipboard. No ⌘C, no right-click.

Select, and it's copied

Drag across text, double-click a word or triple-click a paragraph. Copy on Select copies it straight away as plain text, and a small "Copied" notice confirms it.

Out of your way when you don't want it

Hold Option while you select and nothing is copied. Turn it off for a single website, or everywhere, from the toolbar button. Single characters and stray clicks are ignored, so your clipboard isn't overwritten by accident.

Careful with what you type

Text you select inside text boxes and editors isn't copied unless you turn that on. Password fields are never copied.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no network requests. Copied text goes to your Mac's clipboard and nowhere else. Privacy policy.

Requires macOS 13 or later and Safari.
