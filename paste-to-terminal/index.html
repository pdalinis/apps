Paste to Terminal

for iTerm2 · Safari extension for Mac

Select text in Safari and send it straight into your iTerm2 windows and coding agents. No more copy, switch, paste.

Right-click, pick a tab, done

Select text on any page, right-click, and choose Paste to iTerm2. The menu lists your open iTerm2 windows and tabs; the tab you're working in is marked ✓. The text lands in that tab as a single paste, line breaks and indentation intact.

Never runs anything

Text arrives as a paste, ready for you to read and press Return. Hidden control characters that could end the paste early or press Enter for you are removed first, so a web page can't make your terminal run a command.

Strips hidden text

Some pages hide text you can't see, for example white-on-white or zero-size text, which comes along when you copy. That's a known way to slip instructions to coding agents. Paste to Terminal sends only the text you can see, and shows you what it removed.

Works with your setup

Paste into Claude Code, Codex, shells, REPLs, or anything else running in an iTerm2 tab. If you use tmux or another multiplexer inside a tab, the text goes to its active pane.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no network requests. What you send goes from Safari to iTerm2 on your Mac and nowhere else. Privacy policy.

Requires macOS 13 or later, Safari, and iTerm2. Not affiliated with iTerm2.
