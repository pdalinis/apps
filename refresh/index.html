Refresh

Auto Reload Pages · Safari extension for Mac

Refresh reloads a page on a schedule you choose, and keeps doing it reliably.

Set it and forget it

Pick 30 seconds, 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or any interval up to 7 days. Refresh keeps reloading when the tab is in the background or your Mac's display is off.

Never loses what you're typing

If you're typing on the page when a refresh is due, Refresh waits until you stop.

See it at a glance

The toolbar icon turns green while a tab is refreshing and shows the interval. A small countdown in the corner of the page shows when the next refresh is due.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Refresh sends nothing anywhere. Privacy policy.
