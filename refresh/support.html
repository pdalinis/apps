Turning Refresh on

  1. Open the Refresh app once.
  2. In Safari, choose Safari → Settings → Extensions and tick Refresh.
  3. Allow it on the websites you want to refresh.
  4. Click the Refresh icon in the toolbar, pick an interval and press Start refreshing.

Common questions

Why does a short interval slow down when my screen is off?
Safari limits background timers. Intervals under 30 seconds are exact while the tab is on screen; when the display is off they slow to every 30 seconds. Intervals of 30 seconds or more keep their schedule.

The page didn't refresh when I expected.
If you were typing on the page, Refresh waits until you stop. You can turn this off in the Refresh popup with "Wait while I'm typing".

How do I know it's running?
The toolbar icon is green and shows the interval while a tab is refreshing, and grey when it isn't. A small countdown also appears in the corner of the page.

Why did it stop?
Refresh stops when you go to a different page, close the tab or quit Safari, so it never keeps reloading a page you didn't choose. Use "Start automatically on this page" to have it start again whenever you open that page.
