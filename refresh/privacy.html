Refresh – Auto Reload Pages · Last updated 2026-10-12

Refresh is a Safari extension that reloads web pages on a schedule you choose.

What Refresh collects

Nothing. Refresh does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

To do its job, Refresh keeps a small amount of information in Safari's local extension storage, on your Mac only:

  • the refresh interval you chose for each website;
  • whether "Wait while I'm typing" is on for each website;
  • the pages you asked to start refreshing automatically (website and page address, without any query string);
  • a short log of recent refreshes (times, and the browser's internal number for the tab), so you can check it is working.

This information never leaves your Mac. Refresh makes no network requests of its own; the only thing it asks Safari to do is reload the page you chose.

Website access

Safari asks you to allow Refresh on websites. Refresh needs this to show the countdown on the page and to notice when you are typing, so it can wait before reloading. It does not read, copy or send the content of any page.

Deleting your data

Remove the Refresh app, or turn the extension off and clear its website data in Safari Settings, and everything above is deleted.
